# Vibe Launcher hides its Inputs button.
#
# Format: packages under [always] are disabled; [ask:yes] / [ask:no] groups are asked about, and
# Enter takes the yes/no in brackets. Nothing is ever uninstalled; ./restore.sh undoes it all.

[always] Telemetry, feedback and the beta programme
com.nvidia.stats

com.nvidia.shieldbeta
